## Why This K-Food Hack Works
Casein protein in dairy binds directly to capsaicin molecules—the compound responsible for Buldak's fiery Scoville rating—and washes them away from your taste buds. By swapping the standard 8 tablespoons of cooking water for whole milk and cheese, you neutralize the burn while creating an ultra-creamy sauce.

## Grocery Substitutes for US/UK Kitchens
* **No Whole Milk?** Use heavy cream diluted with water (1:1 ratio) or unsweetened oat milk for a dairy-free alternative.
* **Best Cheese Match:** Low-moisture shredded mozzarella gives the iconic Korean street-food cheese pull, while Kraft American cheese creates the smoothest emulsion.
