## Why This Hack Works
Adding mayonnaise to instant ramen might sound unconventional, but the oil and egg-yolk emulsifiers in Kewpie mayo create an ultra-glossy, rich broth without curdling when mixed with hot starchy water. Combined with Shin Ramyun's beefy spice profile, it mimics high-end Japanese mazemen.

## Grocery Substitutes for US/UK Kitchens
* **No Kewpie Mayo?** Regular American mayonnaise works, but add a tiny pinch of sugar and rice vinegar to match Kewpie's signature umami sweetness.
