## Why This Hack Works
Gochujang is fundamentally built on three pillars: fermented depth (soybean), sweetness, and chili heat. Miso provides the deep, fermented umami funk found in authentic Korean jang, while Sriracha and brown sugar replicate the sharp garlic-chili punch and sticky sweetness.

## Grocery Substitutes for US/UK Kitchens
* **No Miso?** You can substitute with a thick soy sauce paste or even standard tahini in a pinch, though miso yields the most authentic fermented flavor profile.
