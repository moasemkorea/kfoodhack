  - "Crack 2 eggs into a shallow bowl, season with a pinch of salt, and beat well."

  - "Heat oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat and pan-fry the slices for 2 minutes on each side until golden and crispy."

## Why This Hack Works
Defrosted frozen kimbap can sometimes have a slightly dry rice texture. Coating each slice in egg batter and pan-frying seals in the moisture of the sesame oil and vegetables while adding a savory, golden crust reminiscent of traditional Korean holiday dishes (*Jeon*).

## Grocery Substitutes for US/UK Kitchens
* **Dipping Sauce:** Serve alongside a quick mix of soy sauce, a splash of rice vinegar, and a dash of toasted sesame seeds.
